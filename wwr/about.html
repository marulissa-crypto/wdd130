About White Water Rafting

Pioneering thrilling and safe river adventures since 1998.

Our History

Founded in 1998, Whitewater River Rafting began as a passion project between two lifelong river guides. Over the last two decades, our fleet has grown from two rafts to a full-service expedition outfitter guiding thousands of adventure seekers every year across scenic wild rivers.
