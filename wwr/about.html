Whitewater Rafting Co.

Our mission is to provide unforgettable, safe, and exhilarating whitewater rafting experiences for adventure lovers of all skill levels while honoring and preserving the natural beauty of our rivers.

History

Founded in 1998 by a small group of passionate river guides, Whitewater Rafting Co. started with just two rafts and a desire to share the beauty of the canyon waters. Over the past two decades, we have safely guided over 50,000 adventurous guests down world-class rapids.
